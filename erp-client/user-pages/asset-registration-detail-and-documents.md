# Asset Registration — viewing an asset and its documents

This guide covers the Asset Registration detail page
(`/asset-registration/{id}/view`).

## Opening the page

From the top menu choose **Registry → Asset Registration**, find the asset in the list (or
use search), and click its row. The detail page opens.

## Reading the record

The asset's fields are grouped into panels so you can see the whole record without
scrolling on a normal screen:

- **Identification** — ID, asset number, asset tag, model and serial numbers, asset
  details.
- **Financials & Dates** — capitalization date, registration date, asset cost, historical
  cost.
- **Classification & Location** — asset category, dealer, main service outlet, designated
  users, other service outlets.
- **Acquisition & Procurement** — acquiring transaction, other related settlements,
  payment invoices, purchase orders, delivery notes, job sheets.
- **Related Documents** — supporting files (see below), the comments attachment, and asset
  warranties.
- **Supporting Data** — placeholders, universally unique mappings, asset accessories,
  remarks.

On narrow screens the panels stack into a single column.

## Opening a supporting document

The **Related Documents** panel lists every business document linked to the asset. This
includes documents attached to:

- the asset itself,
- the asset's **Acquiring Transaction** (the settlement that paid for it), and
- any **other related settlements**.

Duplicates are removed, so each document appears once.

To view a document:

1. Click its button in the document list. The document loads into the preview frame below
   the list.
2. To see it full size, click **open in new tab** in the preview header. The file opens in
   a new browser tab.

If no documents are linked, the panel shows
"No related business documents were found."

## Other actions

- **Back** returns to the previous page.
- **Edit** opens the asset for editing and starts the register-update workflow.
- **Copy** starts a new asset pre-filled from this one.
